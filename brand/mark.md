# Mark

The mark is a slingshot's fork with the stone just above it, already let go: `mark.svg`, drawn on a 24 by 28 grid. `mark.png` is the same mark on paper at 512px, for avatars.

## Rules

- **Draw the mark in ink on paper, or in paper on ink.** Because the mark is part of the type system, not a colour moment.
- **Never recolour, rotate, stretch or outline the mark.** Because each change reads as a different brand.
- **Keep it at least 12px tall on screen.** Because below that the stone merges into the fork.
- **Give it clear space of at least half its height on every side.** Because the mark is small and loses its shape when text crowds it.
- **Pair it with the word "Slingshot" in Geist, never in the serif.** Because the serif is reserved for the statement.
